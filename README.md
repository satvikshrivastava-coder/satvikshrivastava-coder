# 💫 About Me:
I am a 2nd-year B.Voc IoT Engineering student at DEI Agra, specializing in Embedded Firmware and Edge AI. My primary career objective is direct employment in Japan's embedded systems sector, aligning hardware-level engineering with the Society 5.0 initiative. My technical focus is on bare-metal C, microcontroller state machines, hardware interrupts, and real-time execution constraints.<br><br>Currently, I am participating in the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) and advancing my hardware foundation through Digital Electronics. Recently, I finalized an ATmega328P Smart Security System utilizing non-blocking timing. I also bridge hardware with software via recent completions of the AICTE/Edunet AI internship (AI Placement Agent) and collaborative hackathons (Autyug), proving my ability to integrate AI concepts into practical systems.
